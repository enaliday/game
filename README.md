What Is It & Who Is It For?

Whether you're a rhythm game pro, a dancing enthusiast, or just looking for a fun way to spend your time, Snowfall Village is for YOU! How to Play

Getting started is super easy! Just click the link, and BOOM, you're in! No complicated setup needed. Upload a picture, choose your game mode, and jump straight into the action!

Choose Your Dance Style:

Rhythm Mode: Got good timing? Hit those arrow keys on beat, Friday Night Funkin' style! Don't mess up, or your opponent might just cook you.

Dance Mode: Think you have the moves? Turn on your camera, follow the moves on screen, and show everyone what you've got, Just Dance style!
